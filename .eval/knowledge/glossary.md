# Glossary

_Terms this company uses in a specific way, as their own material defines them._

## Draft

The first version the blog writer returns, before a person has edited it; a draft is never published on its own.

> "Draft means the first version the blog writer returns, before a person has edited it; a draft is never published on its own."
>
> — `glossary.md`


## Light edits

Changes to fewer than a third of a draft's sentences; more than that counts as a rewrite in the success measure.

> "Light edits means changes to fewer than a third of a draft's sentences; more than that counts as a rewrite in our success measure."
>
> — `glossary.md`


## Otto

The chat agent that plans multi-step content tasks before acting; the only workflow that can chain several steps together.

> "Otto is defined as the chat agent that plans multi-step content tasks before acting; it is the only workflow that can chain several steps together."
>
> — `glossary.md`


## Studio

The single web workspace where all five workflows live; "open the studio" means the app, not a physical room.

> "Studio refers to the single web workspace where all five workflows live; when someone says "open the studio" they mean the app, not a physical room."
>
> — `glossary.md`


## Voice

The brand persona every workflow writes in; set once per team and shared across all workflows.

> "Voice refers to the brand persona every workflow writes in; it is set once per team and shared across all workflows."
>
> — `glossary.md`


## Workflow

One of the five things the studio does: blog writing, chat with Otto, summarizing, translating or captioning.

> "Workflow means one of the five things the studio does: blog writing, chat with Otto, summarizing, translating or captioning."
>
> — `glossary.md`
