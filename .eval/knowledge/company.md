# The company

Otto Content Studio is a small Copenhagen software company that sells a per-seat AI workspace bundling five content workflows—blog writing, a chat agent, summarizing, translating and captioning—to weekly-publishing marketing teams.

> "Otto Content Studio is a small software company in Copenhagen."

> — `company-overview.md`

> "We sell to marketing teams of two to twenty people at companies that publish at least weekly."

> — `company-overview.md`
