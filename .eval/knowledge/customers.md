# Customers

_Who uses the product, and the outcome each segment came for._

## Content marketers

The daily users who arrive with a topic and tone and need a publishable blog draft within the hour that can go out with light edits instead of a rewrite.

> "Content marketers are the daily users."
>
> — `customers.md`

> "They arrive with a topic and a tone and need a publishable draft within the hour, because the editorial calendar does not move."
>
> — `customers.md`

> "A content marketer judges the blog writer on one thing: whether the draft can go out with light edits instead of a rewrite."
>
> — `customers.md`

> "The blog writer produces long-form drafts in the team's own brand voice from just a topic and a tone, so the first draft already sounds like the company instead of like a template."
>
> — `positioning.md`


## Social media managers

Use the caption generator many times a day, often from a phone, to get an on-brand caption that fits the platform and ends with something a reader can act on.

> "Social media managers use the caption generator many times a day, often on a phone between meetings."
>
> — `customers.md`

> "They need a caption that sounds like the brand, fits the platform, and ends with something a reader can act on."
>
> — `customers.md`

> "The caption generator writes punchy, on-brand social captions from a one-line description, with one emoji and a call to action, so a social media manager can post from their phone."
>
> — `positioning.md`


## Team leads (buyers)

Use the summarizer to turn long documents into five bullets before meetings and want output that never embarrasses the brand, nothing sent without a human, and one team voice.

> "Team leads rarely generate content themselves."
>
> — `customers.md`

> "They use the summarizer to turn long documents into five bullets before a meeting, and they read the chat history to see what the team asked Otto to do."
>
> — `customers.md`

> "A team lead is the buyer."
>
> — `customers.md`

> "They care that the output never embarrasses the brand, that nothing is sent without a human seeing it, and that the whole team works from one voice."
>
> — `customers.md`
