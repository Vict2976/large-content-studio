# A blog draft, its headline and subheadings must never contain hedging words such as "might", "perhaps", "maybe" or "it depends".

Verifiable by a machine: **yes**.

> "The writer must never hedge, so words like "might", "perhaps", "maybe" and "it depends" cannot appear in a draft, its headline or its subheadings."
