# Otto Content Studio

Otto Content Studio is a small software company in Copenhagen. We build one AI workspace for marketing teams who publish every week and are tired of switching between five tools to do it.

Our product bundles five workflows into one studio: a blog writer, a chat agent called Otto, a summarizer, a translator and a caption generator. Every workflow runs on the same content-marketing persona, so a team gets one voice across everything it ships.

We sell to marketing teams of two to twenty people at companies that publish at least weekly. We are not a general chat product and we do not sell to consumers.

The company is eleven people. Revenue comes from a per-seat subscription billed monthly, and a team's seats share one API key that their administrator issues from the studio.
