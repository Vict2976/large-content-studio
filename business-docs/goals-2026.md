# What good looks like in 2026

Our goal for 2026 is that a marketing team publishes twice as much with the same headcount, and that the extra volume reads as if the same person wrote all of it.

We want to be the tool a team opens first in the morning, so that the studio is where work starts rather than where it is pasted afterwards.

Success means a draft leaves the blog writer and is published with light edits in at least four cases out of five. A draft that gets rewritten is a failure we want to see and fix, not a success we count.

We need to keep the agent trustworthy. Otto plans before it acts, and every plan is shown to the user before anything runs, in order to keep a person in control of what gets sent.

The aim for translation is fidelity of tone, not just of words. A translated caption should land the same way in Danish as it did in English.
