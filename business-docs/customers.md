# Who uses Otto, and what they came for

## Content marketers

Content marketers are the daily users. They arrive with a topic and a tone and need a publishable draft within the hour, because the editorial calendar does not move.

A content marketer judges the blog writer on one thing: whether the draft can go out with light edits instead of a rewrite. If they rewrite more than a third of it, they stop using it and go back to a blank document.

## Social media managers

Social media managers use the caption generator many times a day, often on a phone between meetings. They need a caption that sounds like the brand, fits the platform, and ends with something a reader can act on.

## Team leads

Team leads rarely generate content themselves. They use the summarizer to turn long documents into five bullets before a meeting, and they read the chat history to see what the team asked Otto to do.

A team lead is the buyer. They care that the output never embarrasses the brand, that nothing is sent without a human seeing it, and that the whole team works from one voice.
