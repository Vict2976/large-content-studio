# Words we use in a specific way

Studio refers to the single web workspace where all five workflows live; when someone says "open the studio" they mean the app, not a physical room.

Workflow means one of the five things the studio does: blog writing, chat with Otto, summarizing, translating or captioning.

Otto is defined as the chat agent that plans multi-step content tasks before acting; it is the only workflow that can chain several steps together.

Draft means the first version the blog writer returns, before a person has edited it; a draft is never published on its own.

Voice refers to the brand persona every workflow writes in; it is set once per team and shared across all workflows.

Light edits means changes to fewer than a third of a draft's sentences; more than that counts as a rewrite in our success measure.
